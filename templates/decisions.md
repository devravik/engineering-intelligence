# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

Record significant, deliberate technical choices and trade-offs here to prevent AI agents and new contributors from re-introducing rejected patterns.

---

## ADR-001: [Title of First Architecture Decision]
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** [Accepted / Superseded]
- **Context:** [What problem or dilemma were we facing?]
- **Decision:** [What did we decide to do?]
- **Rationale:** [Why did we choose this approach over alternatives?]
- **Consequences:** [What constraints does this impose on future code?]
